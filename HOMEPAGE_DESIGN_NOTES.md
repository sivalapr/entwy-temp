# Homepage design direction

Audience: business and technology leaders evaluating ENTWY for AI, software, and automation projects. Primary action: start a project. Secondary action: understand capabilities.

Skills installed and read: Anthropic frontend-design and Vercel web-design-guidelines, in ~/.codex/skills.

Palette: warm white #FFFEFB, coffee ink #201515, body grey #605D52, action orange #FF4F00, slate #627D9E, pale blue #DAE7F1. The user rejected the green gradient; replace it with a restrained mineral-blue gradient. Keep orange for actions.

Type: Plus Jakarta Sans headings, Inter body. Sentence case. No decorative eyebrows, blinking dots, sparkles, or italic headline fragments.

Layout: left-aligned proposition with an interactive workflow example; capabilities; team use cases; governance; delivery; insights; contact. Solutions, work, and industries retain their dedicated destinations in navigation.

    Proposition                Workflow example
    Capabilities               Select and explore
    Team use cases             Four team cards
    Governance                 Execution example
    Delivery                   Five chronological stages
    Insights                   Three articles
    Start a project            Contact invitation

Plan critique: avoid repeating the rejected catalogue of card grids. Shorten the homepage and use its visual space to explain ENTWY. Maintain the existing brand typography instead of adding another font for novelty. Use one background family across the homepage; the user's latest rejection of green supersedes the earlier green recipe.

UX: label examples honestly instead of displaying unsupported live statistics; use native buttons, accessible tab relationships and arrow-key navigation, visible focus, responsive sizing, and reduced-motion support.
